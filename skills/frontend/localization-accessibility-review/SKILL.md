# Skill: Localization and Accessibility Review
## Goal
Check the agreed English/Spanish prototype experience and basic accessibility expectations.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the screens and languages in scope.
2. Check navigation, labels, buttons, errors, empty/loading states, tooltips, and accessibility names in both languages.
3. Check that text expansion does not break layouts and that switching language preserves the expected context as specified.
4. Review keyboard navigation, focus visibility/order, form labels, semantic roles, contrast, and meaningful alternative text where applicable.
5. Check date/number formatting only against approved locale rules.
6. Record screenshots and reproducible steps without exposing sensitive data.
7. Send domain-specific translations to the product owner for review.

## Output
Localization/accessibility checklist, findings, evidence, and scope limitations.

## Guardrails
Do not claim full WCAG compliance from a lightweight review. Do not invent translation or locale-formatting decisions.
