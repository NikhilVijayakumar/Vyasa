# Skill: User Story and Acceptance Criteria Design
## Goal
Create small, testable stories that support SDD implementation and QA traceability.

## Procedure
1. Link each story to one or more confirmed requirement IDs.
2. Use a clear role, capability, and business outcome.
3. Keep stories small enough to implement and verify independently.
4. Write acceptance criteria using observable Given/When/Then outcomes or a clear equivalent.
5. Cover success, validation/error, permissions, empty/loading states, data integrity, and localization where relevant.
6. Identify dependencies, exclusions, data needs, and open questions.
7. Ensure acceptance criteria do not introduce unapproved business rules.
8. Include explicit negative cases when the behaviour is specified.

## Project areas
People Assignment to Projects; Staffing Dashboard; Admin Backoffice; People & Skills; Opportunity Forecasting.

## Output
Story ID, title, description, requirement links, acceptance criteria, scope (Prototype/MVP/Later), dependencies, and open questions.

## Guardrails
Do not make every possible enhancement a requirement. Separate must-have scope from suggestions. Request product-owner review for business rules.
