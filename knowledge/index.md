# Vyasa Knowledge

Durable engineering knowledge about Vyasa, in [Open Knowledge Format v0.2](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md).
It is independent of requirements, OpenSpec changes, and code: those link here, never the reverse.
Rules for this bundle are in `profile.json`; `python3 scripts/verify_knowledge.py` enforces them.

# Sections

* [Product](product/) - Vision, scope, and the phased roadmap
* [Architecture](architecture/) - System structure, interfaces, storage, scanning, sync, and security
* [Concepts](concepts/) - Domain ideas that the rest of the knowledge builds on
* [Decisions](decisions/) - Durable architectural decisions (ADRs)
* [Practices](practices/) - How we build and verify Vyasa
* [Technologies](technologies/) - Formats and tools Vyasa detects or builds on
